# Notices and third-party content

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies only to this repository's original scripts,
templates, and build tooling. It does not grant rights to third-party content.

- Chinese Football Association pages, review text, and review videos remain the property of their original rights holders.
- The `uefa.html` page indexes the UEFA "Clear Line" refereeing case library: case titles, official explanations, and links belong to UEFA; videos remain on UEFA's own player and are not copied or redistributed by this site.
- The `pro.html` page indexes weekly refereeing review articles by the Professional Referee Organization (PRO, proreferees.com): titles, dates, series metadata, and official links only; article texts and videos remain on PRO's own site and are not copied or redistributed by this site. U.S. Soccer (USSF) refereeing content is linked as a guide only.
- The `conmebol.html` page indexes CONMEBOL's "Situación de Análisis VAR" cases: structured metadata (match, date, venue, situation, minute) and official links only; case videos remain on CONMEBOL's official YouTube and are not copied or redistributed by this site.
- The `intl.html` page is a hand-curated navigation guide to refereeing review programmes published by other federations (Scotland, Croatia, Ukraine, CONMEBOL, England/PGMOL, Mexico, Türkiye, Japan, Russia): entry links and usage notes only; all programme content belongs to its respective publisher and is not copied or redistributed by this site.
- The `rap.html` page is a navigation guide to the UEFA "Refereeing Assistance Programme" (RAP): edition metadata and links only; RAP content itself stays behind UEFA's registration platform and is not copied or redistributed by this site.
- The `rfef.html` page is a Chinese translation of the Spanish RFEF/CTA refereeing manual "Criterios Arbitrales" (card.rfef.es). The manual text, criteria, and case videos belong to the RFEF. Case videos are mirrored locally under `site/videos/rfef/` (git-ignored) for personal offline study only; the page falls back to the official direct links whenever a local copy is missing.
- The `weekly.html` page indexes episodes of seven official refereeing-review programmes published on YouTube by the Scottish FA, the Turkish Football Federation, the J.League, Sky Sports/PGMOL, the Mexican Comisión de Árbitros (FMF), the Argentine Liga Profesional de Fútbol (AFA), and the Russian Football Union (РФС ТВ): episode titles, dates, durations, view counts, and official descriptions are metadata only, and every episode links to the official YouTube watch page. YouTube videos cannot be redistributed under YouTube's Terms of Service, so none are downloaded, embedded, or copied by this site; Chinese episode notes are curated educational annotations of this project.
- The `ifab.html` page is a Chinese translation of The IFAB's "Video Assistant Referee (VAR) protocol" section of the Laws of the Game (theifab.com), including its official FAQs and companion protocol/guideline pages (temporary dismissals, return substitutes, "only the captain", throw-in and goal-kick countdown, concussion substitutions, off-field treatment, time-limited substitutions). The original text belongs to The IFAB; the translation is an independent educational annotation and the page links to the official protocol page and IFAB resources only.
- The `hns.html` page indexes the Croatian Football Federation's (HNS) "Sudačka analiza" per-round refereeing analyses (hns.family): incident metadata (match, minute, type), the federation's own verdict statements, and official YouTube video links are reproduced for reference; the Chinese translations are educational annotations of this project. Croatian original texts and videos belong to the HNS and are not redistributed.
- The `weekly.html` and `quiz.html` pages additionally reference the Ukrainian Association of Football (UAF) Referees' Committee round commentaries (uaf.ua, metadata and official article links only) and quote The IFAB's VAR protocol FAQs as quiz scenarios with Chinese translations; all remain the property of their original publishers.
- The simplified `rules.html` content is generated from The IFAB's Traditional Chinese Laws of the Game 2026/27. The IFAB owns the original rules and related rights.
- Club crests are collected from public sources and remain subject to the rights of the relevant clubs and source providers.
- Links and citations are retained for educational and research use. Please consult the original publisher for authoritative text and reuse permissions.

This project is an independent educational compilation and is not an official publication of the Chinese Football Association, The IFAB, or UEFA.
