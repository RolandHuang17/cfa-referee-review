1. 原则1. Principles

VAR 协议在尽可能的范围内，符合《竞赛规则》的原则与精神。The VAR protocol, as far as possible, conforms to the principles and philosophy of the Laws of the Game.

只有当比赛/赛事主办方已满足《VAR 手册》规定的全部实施条件，并获得 FIFA 书面许可时，方可使用视频助理裁判（VAR）。The use of video assistant referees (VARs) is only permitted where the match/competition organiser has fulfilled all the implementation requirements as set out in the VAR Handbook , and has received written permission from FIFA.

原则Principles

足球比赛中使用 VAR 基于若干原则——所有原则必须适用于每一场使用 VAR 的比赛（错误判给的角球除外，见「可回看的判定/事件」e 项）。The use of VARs in football matches is based on a number of principles, all of which must apply in every match using VARs (except for incorrectly awarded corner kicks – see point e. under '2. Reviewable decisions/incidents') .

1. 视频助理裁判（VAR）是一名比赛官员，可独立调取比赛画面，但仅在出现与下列事项相关的「清晰而明显的错误」或「严重漏判事件」时协助裁判员：1. A video assistant referee (VAR) is a match official, with independent access to match footage, who may assist the referee only in the event of a 'clear and obvious error' or 'serious missed incident' in relation to:

a. 进球 / 未进球a. Goal/no goal

b. 点球 / 不是点球b. Penalty/no penalty

c. 直接红牌（不包括明显错误的第二张警告）c. Direct red Red card ( not including a clearly incorrect second caution)

d. 错罚对象（裁判员向犯规队的错误球员出示黄牌或红牌时）d. Mistaken identity (when the referee cautions or sends off the wrong player of the offending team )

2. 裁判员必须始终作出决定——不允许「不作决定」再借助 VAR 作出决定；「允许比赛继续」的决定同样可以回看。2. The referee must always make a decision, i.e. the referee is not permitted to give 'no decision' and then use the VAR to make the decision; a decision to allow play to continue after an alleged offence can be reviewed.

3. 只有当视频回看清楚表明原判属于「清晰而明显的错误」时，才更改裁判员的原判。3. The original decision given by the referee will not be changed unless the video review clearly shows that the decision was a 'clear and obvious error'.

4. 只有裁判员可以发起「回看」；VAR（及其他比赛官员）只能向裁判员「建议」回看。4. Only the referee can initiate a 'review'; the VAR (and other match officials) can only recommend a 'review' to the referee.

5. 最终决定始终由裁判员作出——或依据 VAR 提供的信息，或在裁判员完成「场上回看」（OFR）之后。5. The final decision is always taken by the referee, either based on information from the VAR or after the referee has undertaken an 'on-field review' (OFR).

6. 回看过程没有时间限制，因为准确比速度更重要。6. There is no time limit for the review process as accuracy is more important than speed.

7. 球员与球队官员不得围住裁判员，也不得试图影响是否回看、回看过程或最终决定。7. The players and team officials must not surround the referee or attempt to influence if a decision is reviewed, the review process or the final decision.

8. 回看过程中裁判员必须保持「可见」，以确保透明。8. The referee must remain 'visible' during the review process to ensure transparency.

9. 若事件发生后比赛继续、随后才回看，则在事件发生至回看期间已作出/应作出的纪律处分不予取消（即使原判被更改）——因「停止或干扰有希望的进攻」或 DOGSO 被警告/罚令出场的情形除外。9. If play continues after an incident which is then reviewed, any disciplinary action taken/required during the post-incident period is not cancelled, even if the original decision is changed (except a caution/sending-off for stopping or interfering with a promising attack or DOGSO).

10. 若比赛已停止并重新开始，裁判员不得再发起「回看」——错罚对象情形，或与暴力行为、向他人吐口水、咬人、使用极其攻击性/侮辱性/辱骂性言行相关的潜在罚令出场事件除外。10. If play has stopped and been restarted, the referee may not undertake a 'review' except for a case of mistaken identity or for a potential sending-off offence relating to violent conduct, spitting, biting or extremely offensive, insulting and/or abusive action(s).

11. 可回看事件前后的比赛时段，由《竞赛规则》与 VAR 协议确定。11. The period of play before and after an incident that can be reviewed is determined by the Laws of the Game and VAR protocol.

12. 由于 VAR 会自动「核查」每一情况/决定，教练员或球员无需申请「回看」。12. As the VAR will automatically 'check' every situation/decision, there is no need for coaches or players to request a 'review'.

2. 可回看的判定/事件2. Reviewable decisions/incidents

裁判员仅可在四类改变比赛进程的判定/事件上获得 VAR 协助（若赛事启用 e 项的角球判罚则为第五类）。在所有这些情形中，VAR 仅在裁判员已作出（第一/原始）决定（包括允许比赛继续）之后使用，或在比赛官员漏看/未看到严重事件时使用。The referee may receive assistance from the VAR only in relation to four five categories of match-changing decisions/incidents. In all these situations, the VAR is only used after the referee has made a (first/original) decision (including allowing play to continue), or if a serious incident is missed/not seen by the match officials.

只有出现「清晰而明显的错误」时才会更改裁判员的原判（包括裁判员依据其他比赛官员提供的信息〔如越位〕作出的决定）。The referee`s original decision will not be changed unless there was a 'clear and obvious error' (this includes any decision made by the referee based on information from another match official e.g. offside).

出现潜在「清晰而明显的错误」或「严重漏判事件」时，可回看的判定/事件类别为：The categories of decision/incident which may be reviewed in the event of a potential 'clear and obvious error' or 'serious missed incident' are:

a. 进球 / 未进球a. Goal/no goal

  • 进攻队在进球过程或进球前的犯规（手球、犯规、越位等）attacking team offence in the build-up to or scoring of the goal (handball, foul, offside etc.)
  • 进球前球已出界ball out of play prior to the goal
  • 进球 / 未进球判定本身goal/no goal decisions
  • 罚球点球时守门员和/或主罚队员犯规；或攻守队员过早进入禁区，并在球从球门柱、横梁或守门员弹回时直接参与比赛offence by goalkeeper and/or kicker at the taking of a penalty kick or encroachment by an attacker or defender who becomes directly involved in play if the penalty kick rebounds from the goalpost, crossbar or goalkeeper

b. 点球 / 不是点球b. Penalty kick/no penalty kick

  • 点球事件发生前进攻队的犯规（手球、犯规、越位等）attacking team offence in the build-up to the penalty incident (handball, foul, offside etc.)
  • 事件发生前球已出界ball out of play prior to the incident
  • 犯规地点（罚球区之内或之外）location of offence (inside or outside the penalty area)
  • 错误判给的点球penalty kick incorrectly awarded
  • 漏判的点球犯规penalty kick offence not penalised

c. 直接红牌（不包括第二张黄牌/警告）c. Direct red Red cards (not second yellow card/caution)

  • DOGSO——破坏明显进球得分机会（尤其是犯规地点与其他队员位置）DOGSO (especially position of offence and positions of other players)
  • 严重犯规（或鲁莽的抢截)serious foul play (or reckless challenge)
  • 暴力行为、咬人或向他人吐口水violent conduct, biting or spitting at another person
  • 使用攻击性、侮辱性或辱骂性言行using offensive, insulting or abusive action(s)
  • 明显错误的第二张警告clearly incorrect second caution

d. 错罚对象（红牌或黄牌）d. Mistaken identity (red or yellow card)

若裁判员处罚了犯规但明显认错了犯规球员，向犯规（被判罚）队的错误球员出示了黄牌或红牌，则只能回看犯规球员的身份；犯规本身不可回看——除非其涉及进球、点球事件或直接红牌。If the referee penalises an offence but has clearly misidentified the player who committed that offence and then gives the wrong player from the offending (penalised) team a yellow or red card , only the identity of the offender can be reviewed ; the actual offence itself cannot be reviewed unless it relates to a goal, penalty incident or direct red card .

e. 明显错误判给的角球——前提是该决定可以立即更改且不延误重新开始比赛（赛事可选）e. Clearly incorrectly awarded corner kick if the decision can be changed immediately and without delaying the restart (competition option)

3. 实务安排3. Practicalities

比赛中使用 VAR 涉及以下实务安排：The use of VARs during a match involves the following practical arrangements:

  • VAR 在视频操作室（VOR）内观看比赛，由一名或多名助理视频助理裁判（AVAR）协助The VAR watches the match in the video operation room (VOR) assisted by one or more assistant VARs (AVARs)
  • 根据机位数量与其他因素，可配置多名 AVAR 与一名或多名回放操作员（RO）Depending on the number of camera angles and other considerations, there may be more than one AVAR and one or more replay operators (ROs)
  • 比赛期间只有获授权人员方可进入 VOR，或与 VAR/AVAR/RO 通话Only authorised persons are allowed to enter the VOR or communicate with the VAR/AVAR/RO during the match
  • VAR 可独立调取电视转播画面并控制回放The VAR has independent access to, and replay control of, TV broadcast footage
  • VAR 接入比赛官员的通讯系统，能听到他们的一切通话；VAR 只有按下按钮才能对裁判员说话（避免裁判员被 VOR 内的交谈干扰）The VAR is connected to the communication system being used by the match officials and can hear everything they say; the VAR can only speak to the referee by pushing a button (to avoid the referee being distracted by conversations in the VOR)
  • 若 VAR 正忙于「核查」或「回看」，AVAR 可以对裁判员讲话——尤其是需要停止比赛或确保比赛暂不重新开始时If the VAR is busy with a 'check' or a 'review', the AVAR may speak to the referee especially if the game needs to be stopped or to ensure play does not restart
  • 若裁判员决定查看回放画面，VAR 负责选定最佳机位/回放速度；裁判员也可要求其他/追加的机位与速度If the referee decides to view the replay footage, the VAR will select the best angle/replay speed; the referee can request other/additional angles/speeds

4. 程序4. Procedures

最初决定Original decision

  • 裁判员与其他比赛官员必须始终像没有 VAR 一样作出最初决定（包括纪律处分）——「漏判」事件除外The referee and other match officials must always make an initial decision (including any disciplinary action) as if there was no VAR (except for a 'missed' incident)
  • 不允许「不作决定」——那会导致执法软弱/犹豫、「回看」过多，并在技术故障时造成重大问题The referee and other match officials are not permitted to give 'no decision' as this will lead to 'weak/indecisive' officiating, too many 'reviews' and significant problems if there is a technology failure
  • 只有裁判员能作出最终决定；VAR 与其他比赛官员地位相同，只能协助裁判员The referee is the only person who can make the final decision; the VAR has the same status as the other match officials and can only assist the referee
  • 只有在非常清晰的进攻局面——队员即将射门得分，或将直接突入对方罚球区——才可延迟举旗/鸣哨Delaying the flag/whistle for an offence is only permissible in a very clear attacking situation when a player is about to score a goal or has a clear run into/towards the opponents' penalty area
  • 若助理裁判员延迟举旗，当进攻队进球、获判点球/任意球/角球/界外球，或在最初进攻结束后仍控有球权时，必须举旗；其余情形由助理裁判员根据比赛需要自行决定是否举旗If an assistant referee delays a flag for an offence, the assistant referee must raise the flag if the attacking team scores a goal, is awarded a penalty kick, free kick, corner kick or throw-in, or retains possession of the ball after the initial attack has ended; in all other situations, the assistant referee should decide whether or not to raise the flag, depending on the requirements of the game

核查Check

  • VAR 自动使用不同机位与回放速度，「核查」每一个潜在或实际的进球、点球、直接红牌判定/事件，以及错罚对象情形The VAR automatically 'checks' the TV camera footage for every potential or actual goal, penalty or direct red card decision/incident, or a case of mistaken identity, using different camera angles and replay speeds
  • VAR 可以常速和/或慢速核查画面；一般而言，慢速回放只应用于「事实」判断——如犯规/球员位置、身体接触与手球的触球点、球出界（含进球/未进球）；判断犯规「强度」或是否构成手球犯规应使用常速The VAR can 'check' the footage in normal speed and/or in slow motion but, in general, slow motion replays should only be used for facts, e.g. position of offence/player, point of contact for physical offences and handball, ball out of play (including goal/no goal); normal speed should be used for the 'intensity' of an offence or to decide if it was a handball offence
  • 若核查未显示「清晰而明显的错误」或「严重漏判事件」，VAR 通常无需与裁判员通话——这称为「静默核查」；不过，VAR 有时确认「无错漏判」也有助于裁判员/助理裁判员管理球员与比赛If the 'check' does not indicate a 'clear and obvious error' or 'serious missed incident', there is usually no need for the VAR to communicate with the referee – this is a 'silent check'; however, it sometimes helps the referee/assistant referee to manage the players/match if the VAR confirms that no 'clear and obvious error' or 'serious missed incident' occurred
  • 若需为核查延迟重新开始比赛，裁判员以一手食指明确指向耳麦/耳机、另一手臂前伸示意；该手势须保持到核查结束——它表示裁判员正在接收信息（可能来自 VAR 或其他比赛官员）If the restart of play needs to be delayed for a 'check', the referee will signal this by clearly holding a finger to the earpiece/headset and extending the other hand/arm; this signal must be maintained until the 'check' is complete as it announces that the referee is receiving information (which may be from the VAR or another match official)
  • 若核查显示可能存在「清晰而明显的错误」或「严重漏判事件」，VAR 会将信息告知裁判员，由裁判员决定是否发起「回看」If the 'check' indicates a probable 'clear and obvious error' or 'serious missed incident', the VAR will communicate this information to the referee, who will then decide whether or not to initiate a 'review'

回看Review

  • 出现潜在「清晰而明显的错误」或「严重漏判事件」时，裁判员在以下情况可发起「回看」：VAR（或其他比赛官员）建议回看The referee can initiate a 'review' for a potential 'clear and obvious error' or 'serious missed incident' when: the VAR (or another match official) recommends a 'review'
  • 裁判员自己怀疑漏看了严重事件the referee suspects that something serious has been 'missed'
  • 若比赛已停止，裁判员延迟重新开始比赛If play has already stopped, the referee delays the restart
  • 若比赛尚未停止，裁判员在球下次进入中立区域/局面（通常是双方都不处于进攻中）时停止比赛，并做出「电视信号」手势If play has not already stopped, the referee stops play when the ball is next in a neutral zone/situation (usually when neither team is in an attacking move) and shows the 'TV signal'
  • VAR 向裁判员描述电视回放中的内容，裁判员随后：做出「电视信号」手势（如尚未做出），前往裁判回看区查看回放画面——「场上回看」（OFR）——再作出最终决定；其他比赛官员一般不查看回放，除非在特殊情况下受裁判员要求，或者——The VAR describes to the referee what can be seen on the TV replay(s) and the referee then: shows the 'TV signal' (if not already shown) and goes to the referee review area to view replay footage – 'on-field review' (OFR) – before making a final decision. The other match officials will not review the footage unless, in exceptional circumstances, asked to do so by the referee or
  • 依据裁判员自己的判断、VAR 提供的信息以及（适当时的）其他比赛官员的意见，直接作出最终决定——「仅 VAR 回看」makes a final decision based on the referee's own perception and the information from the VAR, and, where appropriate, input from other match officials – 'VAR-only review'
  • 两种回看流程结束时，裁判员必须先做「电视信号」手势，随即给出最终决定At the end of both review processes, the referee must show the 'TV signal' immediately followed by the final decision
  • 主观性决定——如犯规强度、越位干扰、手球考量——适用「场上回看」（OFR）For subjective decisions, e.g. intensity of a foul challenge, interference at offside, handball considerations, an 'on-field review' (OFR) is appropriate
  • 事实性决定——如犯规/球员位置（越位）、触球点（手球/犯规）、地点（罚球区之内或之外）、球出界等——通常适用「仅 VAR 回看」；但若有助于管理球员/比赛或让决定更易被接受（如比赛末段决定胜负的关键判罚），事实性决定也可使用「场上回看」For factual decisions e.g. position of an offence or player (offside), point of contact (handball/foul), location (inside or outside the penalty area), ball out of play etc. a 'VAR-only review' is usually appropriate but an 'on-field review' (OFR) can be used for a factual decision if it will help manage the players/match or 'sell' the decision (e.g. a crucial match-deciding decision late in the game)
  • 裁判员可以要求不同机位/回放速度；一般而言，慢速回放只应用于事实判断（如犯规/球员位置、身体接触与手球的触球点、球出界〔含进球/未进球〕），判断犯规「强度」或是否构成手球应使用常速The referee can request different cameras angles/replay speeds but, in general, slow motion replays should only be used for facts, e.g. position of offence/player, point of contact for physical offences and handball, ball out of play (including goal/no goal); normal speed should be used for the 'intensity' of an offence or to decide if it was a handball offence
  • 与进球、点球/不是点球及 DOGSO 红牌相关的判定/事件，可能需要回看直接导致该判定/事件的进攻阶段——包括运动战中进攻队如何获得球权For decisions/incidents relating to goals, penalty/no penalty and red cards for denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity (DOGSO), it may be necessary to review the attacking phase of play which led directly to the decision/incident; this may include how the attacking team gained possession of the ball in open play
  • 《竞赛规则》不允许在比赛重新开始后更改重新开始方式的决定（角球、界外球等），因此此类决定不可回看The Laws of the Game do not allow restart decisions (corner kicks, throw-ins etc.) to be changed once play has restarted, so they cannot be reviewed
  • 若比赛已停止并重新开始，裁判员只能为错罚对象情形，或与暴力行为、向他人吐口水、咬人、极其攻击性/侮辱性/辱骂性言行相关的潜在罚令出场事件发起「回看」并作出相应纪律处罚If play has stopped and restarted, the referee may only undertake a 'review', and take the appropriate disciplinary sanction, for a case of mistaken identity or for a potential sending-off offence relating to violent conduct, spitting, biting or extremely offensive, insulting and/or abusive action(s)
  • 回看过程应尽快完成，但最终决定的准确性重于速度；因此——且由于部分情形复杂、包含多个可回看点——回看没有最长时限The review process should be completed as efficiently as possible, but the accuracy of the final decision is more important than speed. For this reason, and because some situations are complex with several reviewable decisions/incidents, there is no maximum time limit for the review process

最终决定Final decision

  • 回看结束后，裁判员必须做出「电视信号」手势并宣布最终决定；赛事也可按 FIFA 指南与《VAR 手册》所述，实行由裁判员在 VAR「回看」或长时间「核查」后公开解释并宣布决定的制度When the review process is completed, the referee must show the 'TV signal' and communicate the final decision; competitions may also implement a system in which the referee publicly explains and announces decisions following a VAR 'review' or lengthy VAR 'check', as outlined in the FIFA guidelines and the VAR Handbook
  • 随后裁判员（视情况）作出/更改/撤销纪律处分，并按《竞赛规则》重新开始比赛The referee will then take/change/rescind any disciplinary action (where appropriate) and restart play in accordance with the Laws of the Game

球员、替补队员与球队官员Players, substitutes and team officials

  • VAR 会自动「核查」每一情况/事件，教练员与球员无需申请「核查」或「回看」As the VAR will automatically 'check' every situation/incident, there is no need for coaches or players to request a 'check' or 'review'
  • 球员、替补队员与球队官员不得试图影响或干扰回看过程，包括宣布最终决定时Players, substitutes and team officials must not attempt to influence or interfere with the review process, including when the final decision is communicated
  • 回看期间，球员应留在比赛场地内；替补队员与球队官员应留在比赛场地外During the review process, players should remain on the field of play; substitutes and team officials should remain off the field of play
  • 过度做出「电视信号」手势或进入裁判回看区（RRA）的球员/替补队员/被替换队员/球队官员，将被警告A player/substitute/substituted player/team official who excessively shows the 'TV signal' or enters the RRA will be cautioned
  • 进入视频操作室（VOR）的球员/替补队员/被替换队员/球队官员，将被罚令出场A player/substitute/substituted player/team official who enters the VOR will be sent off

比赛有效性Match validity

原则上，比赛不因以下原因判为无效：In principle, a match is not invalidated because of:

  • VAR 技术故障（与门线技术 GLT 同理）malfunction(s) of the VAR technology (as for goal line technology (GLT))
  • 涉及 VAR 的错误决定（VAR 是比赛官员）wrong decision(s) involving the VAR (as the VAR is a match official)
  • 决定不回看某事件decision(s) not to review an incident
  • 回看了不可回看的情形/决定review(s) of a non-reviewable situation/decision

VAR、AVAR 或回放操作员无法工作时Incapacitated VAR, AVAR or replay operator

《竞赛规则》第 6 章「其他比赛官员」规定：「竞赛规程必须明确写明由谁替换无法开始或继续工作的比赛官员，以及任何相关调整。」在使用 VAR 的比赛中，该条同样适用于回放操作员。Law 6 – The Other Match Officials stipulates: 'Competition rules must state clearly who replaces a match official who is unable to start or continue and any associated changes.' In matches using VARs, this also applies to replay operators.

由于担任视频比赛官员（VMO）/回放操作员需要专门培训与资质，竞赛规程必须包含以下原则：As special training and qualifications are needed to be a video match official (VMO)/replay operator, the following principles must be included in competition rules:

  • 无法开始或继续工作的 VAR、AVAR 或回放操作员，只能由具备该岗位资质者替换A VAR, AVAR or replay operator who is unable to start or continue may only be replaced by someone who is qualified for that role
  • 若无法为 VAR 或回放操作员*找到合格替换者，比赛必须在不使用 VAR 的情况下进行/继续If no qualified replacement can be found for the VAR or replay operator,* the match must be played/continue without the use of VARs
  • 若无法为 AVAR*找到合格替换者，比赛必须在不使用 VAR 的情况下进行/继续——除非在特殊情况下，双方以书面形式同意仅凭 VAR 与回放操作员继续比赛If no qualified replacement can be found for the AVAR,* the match must be played/continue without the use of VARs unless, in exceptional circumstances, both teams agree in writing that the match may be played/continue with only the VAR and replay operator

*若该场配置多名 AVAR/回放操作员，则上述规定不适用。*This does not apply where there is more than one AVAR/replay operator.

官方 FAQ 判例Official FAQs

进攻队员（A 队）踢球后，球意外碰到本方队友（A 队）的手/手臂。裁判员停止比赛，但错误地认定手球犯规系防守队员（B 队）所为，向其出示黄牌（YC）并判给 A 队直接任意球。此时视频助理裁判（VAR）可以告知裁判员「被警告的是非犯规方球员」吗？An attacking player (Team A) kicks the ball which then accidentally touches a hand/arm of their team-mate (Team A). The referee stops play, but wrongly decides that the handball offence was committed by a defending player (Team B), who is punished with a yellow card (YC) and a direct free kick to Team A. May the video assistant referee (VAR) inform the referee that the player from the non-offending team was cautioned?
可以。与第二张黄牌无关的黄牌可以回看，用于确认被判手球犯规的球员身份。本例中防守队员的黄牌被取消，比赛以直接任意球重新开始，判给 B 队。
A YC which is not a second YC can be reviewed to identify the player who committed the handball offence that was penalised. In this case, the YC for the defender is cancelled and play is restarted with a direct free kick to Team B.
进攻队员争抢，防守队员在场地中央倒地假摔（并无身体接触）。裁判员停止比赛，判给防守方任意球，并向进攻队员出示黄牌（YC）——这是其本场第二张黄牌，进而两黄变一红（RC）。VAR 可以就「清晰而明显的错误」告知裁判员并发起回看吗？An attacker challenges a defender, who falls to the ground in the middle of the field and pretends to have been fouled, despite there being no contact. The referee stops play, awards a free kick for the defender&#39;s team and shows a yellow card (YC) to the attacker, which is their second YC in the match and results in a red card (RC). May the video assistant referee (VAR) inform the referee about a &#39;clear and obvious error&#39; so that the incident is reviewed?
可以。VAR 可以协助处理明显错误的第二张黄牌。裁判员回看回放后，进攻队员的第二张黄牌及由此产生的红牌均被取消；防守队员因假摔被出示黄牌，比赛以间接任意球重新开始，判给进攻方。
Yes, the VAR can assist the referee in relation to a clearly incorrect second YC. After the referee reviews the replay footage, the second YC and the subsequent RC for the attacker are cancelled. The defender is shown a YC for simulation and play is restarted with an indirect free kick to the attacker&#39;s team.
一名已领到黄牌的球员实施鲁莽抢截，够得上本场第二张警告；但裁判员在场上没有看到该犯规。VAR 可以以「严重漏判事件」告知裁判员并建议「回看」吗？A player who has already received a yellow card commits a reckless tackle, which would be their second caution in the match; however, the offence is not seen by the referee on the field. May the video assistant referee (VAR) inform the referee about a serious missed incident and recommend a &#39;review&#39;?
不可以。VAR 只能在裁判员出示明显错误的第二张黄牌（进而红牌）时介入，而不能在潜在的第二张黄牌犯规被漏判时介入。
No. The VAR can only intervene when the referee shows a clearly incorrect second yellow card which results in a red card, not when a potential second yellow card offence has been missed.
进攻队员在对方罚球区内倒地，裁判员认为其被防守队员犯规，停止比赛判给点球。裁判员进行场上回看，回放清楚显示进攻队员系假摔——防守队员并未接触进攻队员。裁判员应如何决定？The referee stops play and awards a penalty kick because an attacker falls down in the opponents&#39; penalty area and the referee thinks the attacker was fouled by a defender. The referee undertakes an on-field review and the replay footage clearly shows that the attacker pretends to have been fouled – the defender did not touch the attacker . What is the referee&#39;s decision?
取消点球，改判防守方间接任意球；进攻队员因非体育行为（假摔）被警告（黄牌）。
The penalty kick is cancelled and an indirect free kick is awarded to the defending team. The attacker is cautioned (yellow card) for unsporting behaviour (simulation).
在改变比赛进程的事件之后，比赛已停止并重新开始——裁判员此时还可以发起 VAR「回看」吗？May the referee undertake the VAR &#39;review&#39; if – after a match-changing incident – play has stopped and been restarted?
不可以，仅两种例外：错罚对象情形，或与暴力行为、向他人吐口水、咬人、使用极其攻击性/侮辱性/辱骂性言行相关的潜在罚令出场事件。
No, it is not permitted except for: a case of mistaken identity or for a potential sending-off offence relating to violent conduct, spitting, biting or extremely offensive, insulting and/or abusive action(s).
球员的抢截可能危及对方安全。VAR 因该事件未被裁判员看到而建议场上回看（潜在红牌犯规）。裁判员完成回看后，可以改判警告（黄牌）吗？A player makes a challenge which might endanger the safety of the opponent. The video assistant referee recommends an on-field review for a potential sending-off (red card, RC) offence because this incident was not seen by the referee. May the referee, after undertaking the review, decide to caution (yellow card, YC) the offender?
最终决定必须以回看所见为准——即使回看是为红牌发起的，若裁判员观看回放后认为该犯规只值警告而非罚令出场，就必须出示黄牌。
The final decision(s) must be based on what has been seen – therefore although the review was initiated for a red card offence if the referee&#39;s opinion, after watching the replays, is that the offence was a caution (YC) and not a sending-off (RC), then the referee must issue a YC.
裁判员已判给点球，但 VAR 建议「回看」——因为犯规发生在罚球区外。正确的程序是什么？The referee has awarded a penalty kick, however, the video assistant referee (VAR) recommends a &#39;review&#39; because the foul was committed outside the penalty area. What is the appropriate procedure?
事实性决定（如犯规地点在罚球区内外、越位、球出界等）通常适用「仅 VAR 回看」：只需一次「电视信号」手势——VAR 告知裁判员回放内容，裁判员在给出最终决定前即刻做出该手势。
A &#39;VAR-only review&#39; is usually appropriate for factual decisions e.g. location of an offence (inside/outside the penalty area), offside, ball out of play etc. Only one &#39;TV signal&#39; is needed so the VAR tells the referee what the TV replays show and the referee then makes the &#39;TV signal&#39; immediately before giving the final decision.
防守队员扩大身体面积，在己方罚球区内用手臂触球，裁判员令比赛继续。VAR 将该事件告知裁判员。正确的程序是什么？A defending team player has made their body bigger and touched the ball with their arm inside their own penalty area. The referee allows play to continue. The video assistant referee (VAR) communicates the incident to the referee. What is the correct procedure?
裁判员在球下次进入中立区域/局面时停止比赛，并做出「电视信号」手势。这属于主观性决定，适用「场上回看」（OFR）——裁判员前往裁判回看区查看回放画面后再作出最终决定。
The referee stops play when the ball is next in a neutral zone/situation and shows the &#39;TV signal&#39;. As this is a subjective decision, an &#39;on-field review&#39; (OFR) is appropriate so the referee goes to the referee review area to view replay footage before making a final decision.
裁判员可以「不作决定」，然后借助 VAR 作出决定吗？Is the referee permitted to give &#39;no decision&#39; and then use the VAR to make a decision?
不可以。VAR 只能在裁判员已作出第一决定（包括允许比赛继续）之后使用，或在比赛官员漏看/未看到严重事件时使用。
No. The VAR is only used after the referee has made a first decision (including allowing play to continue), or if a serious incident is missed/not seen by the match officials.
守门员在罚球点球时犯规，随后又扑出射门。这种情形允许使用 VAR 吗？A goalkeeper committed an offence while a penalty kick was taken and then saved a kick. Is the use of VAR allowed in this situation?
允许。罚球点球时守门员和/或主罚队员犯规的，裁判员可以获得 VAR 协助。
Yes. The referee may receive assistance from the VAR in case of offence by the goalkeeper and/or kicker at the taking of a penalty kick.
裁判员判给点球。VAR 回看显示进攻队员假摔（假装被犯规）。裁判员应如何决定？The referee awards a penalty kick. The VAR review shows that an attacking player was guilty of simulation (pretended to be fouled). What is the referee&#39;s decision?
裁判员回看回放后，取消点球并改判防守方间接任意球；假摔的进攻队员必须因非体育行为被警告（黄牌）。
After the referee has reviewed the replay footage, the penalty kick is cancelled and an indirect free kick is awarded to the defending team. The attacking player who was guilty of simulation must be cautioned (yellow card, YC) for unsporting behaviour.
一名球员在对方罚球区内因假摔被警告（黄牌）。该事件随后被回看，回放清楚显示该球员确实被犯规。裁判员应如何决定？A player receives a caution (yellow card, YC) for simulation in the opponents&#39; penalty area. The incident is then reviewed by the referee and the VAR review clearly shows that the player was fouled. What is the referee&#39;s decision?
若裁判员认定并非假摔，则取消黄牌并改判点球；视情况，犯规的防守队员被出示黄牌或红牌。
